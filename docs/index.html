Made for the long game

From the first piece to a growing hoard.

Treasure Log brings your physical holdings into one calm, useful view. Add what you own, understand the fine-metal weight, and choose how you want to track progress.

Practical collection tools, wrapped in a world that feels like yours.

01 / INVENTORY
◈

Track every holding

Keep name, metal, quantity, weight, purity, purchase details, and notes together. See fine-weight totals alongside the details you entered.

GoldSilverPlatinumCopper
02 / PRESETS
▦

Start with a preset

Choose a built-in item template or save your own custom presets for pieces you add again.

03 / PRICES
↗

Choose your reference

Review available market quotes for gold, silver, and platinum. Set a manual price override whenever you prefer your own reference.

Reference values are estimates, not appraisals.
04 / GOALS
◎

Give the journey a marker

Set a collection-value or weight goal and watch your illustrated chest progress as your collection changes.

✦
05 / LOCAL DATA
⌂

Keep your ledger close

Holdings and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Export a JSON backup to save or share using the iOS file and share tools.

06 / RESTORE
↻

Bring a backup back

Restore from a JSON backup with a preview and confirmation before the current collection is replaced.
