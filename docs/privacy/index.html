Privacy policy.

A plain-language explanation of how Treasure Log and this website handle information.

Effective date: October 25, 2026
App: Treasure Log for iOS
Public contact: evenmatterdev@gmail.com

This policy describes current Treasure Log app behavior and this website’s information practices.

Information stored in the app

Treasure Log stores holdings, collection goals, custom presets, cached market quotes, and manual price overrides in local SwiftData storage on your device. Home display preferences use UserDefaults, Apple’s local app-preferences storage.

The app does not require an account and does not operate cloud synchronization. Separately, normal iOS device backups may include Treasure Log’s locally stored data, depending on iOS behavior and your device backup settings. Treasure Log does not explicitly exclude its SwiftData store or UserDefaults preferences from system backups. These system-managed backups are separate from the JSON files you can choose to export from the app.

Market-price requests

Treasure Log automatically requests reference quotes from Gold API for Gold, Silver, and Platinum, and the provisional HG market reference for Copper, when a quote is missing or stale; you can also refresh prices in the app. Each request contains the requested metal symbol in its URL. The app does not send your holdings, purchase costs, goals, notes, presets, or other inventory details in the quote request. Gold API may receive technical request information, including your IP address and request timestamps, as described in its privacy policy. Provider practices can change. No specific retention period for individual requests is stated here.

Gold API provides HG as a provisional Copper market reference, which Treasure Log provisionally interprets as USD per avoirdupois pound. Gold API does not explicitly document the quote unit or underlying benchmark, so this is not verified physical copper spot pricing. Including HG in collection valuations is optional and disabled by default. Manual Copper price overrides are supported and take precedence while set.

Kickstart Exchange, advertising, and tracking

Treasure Log includes Kickstart Exchange SDK 0.6.0 and currently uses Kickstart’s reserved “preview” key. Kickstart says the preview key can load a sample advertisement in development builds and the Simulator. It creates no Exchange app session and records no impressions or clicks; it shows nothing in shipping builds. Production advertising is not active in Treasure Log. The preview integration does not send your holdings, purchase costs, collection goals, notes, or other inventory details.

Loading a preview sample requires a network request to Kickstart Exchange. Kickstart says requests to its service pass through Cloudflare, which processes the connecting IP address as part of delivering network, security, and content-delivery services. Kickstart says it does not store those addresses or use them to identify anyone. Read Kickstart Exchange’s privacy notice for the provider’s description of its practices.

Treasure Log’s app-owned privacy manifest is separate from the Kickstart SDK’s manifest. The app-owned manifest declares no tracking and no data types collected by the app itself. The SDK’s manifest declares Product Interaction and Advertising Data, with purposes that include third-party advertising, developer advertising or marketing, analytics, and app functionality. Kickstart describes these SDK data types as not linked to identity and not used for tracking. Manifest declarations describe declared practices; they do not mean production ads or counted ad interactions are active in Treasure Log’s current preview-only setup, and they do not alone determine the app’s App Store Connect privacy answers.

If production advertising is enabled in a future release, Kickstart says SDK requests may include app-level integration details such as the API key, app identifier, platform, app and build versions, SDK version, development mode where applicable, and the App Store storefront country. Kickstart says storefront country comes from the device’s App Store account and is used for compatibility and app-level accounting, not as a person’s or device’s location or identity. Production can also record app-level advertisement impressions and clicks, which Kickstart says are not associated with individual users or devices.

For production verification, the SDK may send an Apple-signed App Transaction when StoreKit provides one that the app has verified locally. Kickstart says it processes the signed transaction briefly to verify the production app, then immediately discards it without retaining, hashing, logging, correlating, targeting with, or analyzing its transaction or device-verification values. If this evidence is unavailable or cannot be verified, the session is non-counting. Kickstart says app-level advertisement delivery and interaction records are kept for about 30 days; daily app-level statistics have no fixed deletion date. These production behaviors are not active under Treasure Log’s current preview-key integration.

This website itself has no advertising or tracking scripts, analytics scripts, cookie-setting code, forms, or embedded third-party content.

JSON backup export and restore

You can choose to export a JSON backup through the iOS file and sharing interface. The backup can contain holdings, purchase-cost information and currency, notes, your goal, manual price overrides, Home preferences, and custom presets. Cached market quotes are not included in the current backup format. Treasure Log does not encrypt exported backup files.

If you choose to save or share a backup through iCloud Drive, another storage provider, email, or another app, that service may receive or store the file under its own terms and practices. Treasure Log does not control that third-party handling.

Support and privacy contact

You may voluntarily contact us by email at evenmatterdev@gmail.com about support, privacy, or general inquiries. Inquiry messages and information you choose to provide are used to review and respond to support requests. Email providers may process messages as part of delivering their service. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or financial information unless necessary.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages is the intended initial host for this website. When you visit, your browser sends requests to GitHub’s hosting infrastructure. GitHub may process technical information associated with those requests, such as IP addresses, requested pages or resources, browser or device information, and related service logs. See GitHub’s General Privacy Statement for information about its practices. This policy does not state a specific retention period.

The website itself does not add analytics scripts or third-party embeds. That is separate from information a hosting provider may process. If you follow an external link, your browser connects to that service under its own practices.

Audience

Treasure Log is intended for a general audience, primarily adult collectors. It is not directed to children.

Operator, contact, and changes

Treasure Log and this website are operated by an independent individual developer using Evenmatter as a public-facing name. Evenmatter is not a separate legal entity. For privacy, support, or general inquiries, contact evenmatterdev@gmail.com or visit the Support page.

This policy may be updated if the app or website’s information practices change.
